# IG Guard 5.0 — AI risk analysis

Documented: **20 November 2026 (Hong Kong)**. Bundled Terms revision: `2026-11-20-v6`. Earlier version: [4.9-beta.4](4.9-beta.4.md), whose notes still describe the security hardening that 5.0 keeps.

These notes describe this version and its known limits. They do not claim legal clearance, Meta approval, reliable detection of grooming or an absence of defects. The Terms, privacy policy and deletion guide have not been reviewed by a lawyer.

## New since 4.9-beta.4

**AI risk analysis (scores-only mode, optional).** A parent can switch on a mode in which one-to-one Instagram conversations are scored on the child's phone by a local model, only while the phone is charging. The parent receives an account label, a score or "needs review", and times, never message text. The parent can switch it on only after the child's phone reports a valid model, and the child must agree on their own phone; the child can pause or withdraw. See the [Terms](../ig-guard-terms-of-service.md), sections 2A to 2C, and the [privacy policy](../ig-guard-privacy-policy.md).

**The Beacon model inside the app.** The APK published on GitHub Releases for 5.0 contains the model (Beacon, `qwen3-1.7b-beacon-Q4_K_M.gguf`, 1,107,408,736 bytes, Qwen3 1.7B fine-tuned and quantised by the developer, run by llama.cpp). The model file is also published on GitHub Releases as an asset of a separate release (`model-beacon`), so anyone can download it. On first start on a child phone the app copies the model into its private storage after checking free space, size and SHA-256; this needs about 1.1 GB free on top of the installed app. Manual import with the file picker (parent's password) is a recovery path only. The git repository does not contain the model file, and ordinary builds of `main` have none; on them AI risk analysis cannot be used until a model is present. Phones reporting less than about 5 GB of memory, or without an arm64 processor, show the mode as unavailable. Licences: [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](../../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md). How the package is built: [local-model-package.md](../local-model-package.md).

The model was trained and tested on invented conversations, a small number of real conversations supplied by the developer, PAN-12 research conversations and the developer's own ordinary chats. It can be wrong in both directions, cannot reliably tell sexual conversation between adults from grooming, and nothing shows that it reliably detects real grooming. A trained model can sometimes reproduce fragments of its training text. A limited test by the developer found no reproduction of the real training conversations, but it used one setting and one prompt length, left out the PAN-12 conversations, and cannot prove that the model never reproduces training text. Because the model is public, a downloaded copy cannot be recalled. If a child is in immediate danger, call 999 (Hong Kong).

**Two collection methods.** The parent chooses, and the child agrees on their own phone:

- *Automatic capture* reads Instagram notifications and conversation screens. The parent must tick the warning "Automatic capture may breach Instagram's Terms of Use" before the choice is sent, and the child's consent page repeats it (full text in Terms section 2B).
- *Manual upload* reads nothing from Instagram. The teen imports Instagram's own "Download your information" file; only one-to-one chats are kept. There are no real-time keyword or unapproved-contact alerts in this method, and the parent cannot know that the file is complete or unedited.

**Capture hold.** If the child agreed to manual upload and the parent later switches the mode off, the app does not go back to reading Instagram until the child allows it on their own phone. The parent is told that capture is held.

**15-minute background check-in.** The child phone checks in about every 15 minutes (heartbeat and relay poll), even with Notification access and Accessibility both off. Android may delay it.

**Relay: room deletion and 90-day expiry.** A room's registration is deleted after 90 days without a connection from either phone. An authenticated parent can delete a room in the app; the app first sends the child phone a signed release. A deleted or expired room is retired for good, keeping only a content-free marker. Buffered packets still expire seven days after first acceptance.

**Speed fixes found on a real phone.** Decrypted settings and consent receipts are remembered while their sealed text is unchanged, instead of being decrypted through the Keystore on every Instagram screen event (message text never uses this path). The heartbeat check runs behind the 450 ms screen-event throttle again; before this fix it froze the app on a real phone.

## Kept from 4.9-beta.4

Parent access needs native biometric or device-credential authentication. Production parent signing uses non-exportable Keystore identities; if moving an existing key into the Keystore fails, the app keeps using that existing key (stored encrypted by the app, not hardware-protected), shows a warning and retries at each start. Child stop and scope controls, archive and outbox deadlines and compromise re-pairing are unchanged; see the [4.9-beta.4 notes](4.9-beta.4.md) and [storage security](../local-storage-security.md).

## Installing and upgrading

- The release APK with the model is over 1 GB. It installs directly (download it from the Releases page and open it; Android asks to allow installing from that source). Keep about 1.1 GB free beyond the installed app for the first-start copy; a copy of the APK left in Downloads takes another 1.1 GB until deleted.
- **Google Play.** It cannot be uploaded to Google Play as a plain APK: Play limits apps published as APKs to 100 MB. A Play listing would need an Android App Bundle with an asset pack (Play allows up to 1.5 GB per asset pack), which is not built. Source: Play Console Help, "Optimize your app's size and stay within Google Play app size limits", checked 20 November 2026.
- **Upgrading keeps data only if the new APK is signed with the same key** as the installed app. Android refuses an update signed by a different key; uninstalling to get around that deletes pairing, settings, archives and the consent receipt on that phone. See [signing.md](../signing.md).
- Update both phones together.
- People who accepted an earlier Terms revision see a one-time 條款已更新 notice; nobody is asked to accept the Terms again. The child's scores-mode agreement is separate.

## Tested on real phones, and not

Phone test on 20 November 2026, two real devices: the first-start self-install of the model worked; both collection methods worked end to end; on a 2018 phone reporting 5.8 GB, scoring completed with about 1.7 GB peak app memory (one phone, a few short chats; not a guarantee).

Not tested: the natural schedules (the 15-minute check-in and the charging-and-idle scoring job were started by hand, not observed running on their own); the owner's real Instagram export (not imported); any device beyond those two; speed, battery and heat over time; a 1.1 GB import through the file picker; screen readers on real phones. The release workflow step that packages the model into the release APK is planned; these notes do not show that it has run.

## Known limits

Capture depends on what Instagram exposes and can be incomplete or uncertain. Scores are signals, not conclusions. The relay's provider logs, regions and backups remain unverified. The app cannot guarantee protection on a compromised phone or recall copied data. LiquidAI/d1-omni-600M feasibility work is a separate experiment and is not part of the app.
