# IG Guard 5.1 — new look

Documented: **21 November 2026 (Hong Kong)**. Bundled Terms revision: `2026-11-20-v6` (unchanged). Earlier version: [5.0](5.0.md).

5.1 is 5.0 with a new look. Nothing else changed. These notes do not claim legal clearance, Meta approval, reliable detection of grooming or an absence of defects.

## What is new

- **New colours.** Light mode is "Harbour": navy `#1F4E79` on `#F6F8FB`. Dark mode is "Clear Sky": sky blue `#6FB6FF` on `#0B1724`.
- **Risk colours are separate from the brand colour.** Low is `#17703A` (light) and `#66D48F` (dark). Needs review is `#8F5300` and `#F5B84D`. High is `#B3261E` and `#FF8279`. The word or number next to a colour stays. All mapped text and background pairs are at least 4.5:1.
- **Theme change animation.** When you switch theme (the sun or moon button in the header, or Settings), the new theme is uncovered as a circle that grows from the tapped button over about 650 ms. With system animations off, the change is instant.
- **Bottom navigation.** The highlight slides to the tab you select (about 280 ms) instead of jumping.
- **Opening a page.** A page that opens fades in with a small upward slide (about 220 ms): a tab change, opening or leaving a conversation, the alert sub-tabs and the chart mode buttons. A background refresh of the same page does not animate.
- **Remove animations.** With the system "remove animations" setting on, all of this motion is instant.
- **Scores-mode switch row.** It now has the same press ripple as other controls.
- **Corners.** Cards have 14 dp corners. Buttons have 10 dp corners.

## What is unchanged

No feature, screen, setting or behaviour was removed or changed. The wording of the Terms and the consent step are unchanged. The Terms revision stays `2026-11-20-v6`, so nobody is shown a new Terms notice. The Beacon model and the relay are the same. The [5.0 notes](5.0.md) describe the app in full, and all of its limits still apply.

## Installing and upgrading

- *Later version, 23 November 2026 (Hong Kong): see the [5.2 notes](5.2.md).*
- **Upgrading from 5.0 keeps your data.** 5.1 is signed with the same key as 5.0. Update both phones together.
- **Install limit (also true for 5.0).** On phones where Google Play Protect's enhanced fraud protection is active, installing the downloaded APK by opening it from a browser, messaging app or file manager is **blocked**, with no "install anyway" option. The app declares an accessibility service and a notification listener, which that protection blocks. Google says it is rolled out in many markets. It was seen on a Hong Kong tablet on 21 November 2026. Installing over USB with `adb` from a computer is not blocked: `adb install -r IG-Guard-5.1.apk`. There is no Google Play listing.
- The APK is over 1 GB because it contains the model. Space and first-start copy: see the [5.0 notes](5.0.md).

## Tested, and not tested

Tested: 698 unit tests ran. 1 failed, a known Windows-only failure (`RelayOutboxTest > concurrentFirstOpenCannotRaceDatabaseCreation`). Lint reported 0 errors. On two real devices (LG G7+, Android 8, as child; Xiaomi tablet, Android 15, as parent) a debug build without the model was used. Every parent and child tab loaded in both themes. Switching theme from both places worked, including rapid taps. No crash.

Not tested:

- The 5.1 release APK itself on a phone.
- Scoring and scores screens with data on a phone in 5.1. The debug build had no model, so these screens were checked only in test-suite renders.
- Automatic-capture screens with permissions granted.
- Two taps about 50 ms apart on the tablet register as one.
