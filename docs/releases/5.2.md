# IG Guard 5.2 — final review build

Documented: **23 November 2026 (Hong Kong)**. Bundled Terms revision: `2026-11-23-v7`. Earlier version: [5.1](5.1.md).

5.2 helps the child use Instagram's own export, lets the parent ask for a fresh export, fixes security issues and bugs, and changes the light mode. These notes do not claim legal clearance, Meta approval, reliable detection of grooming or an absence of defects. The Terms have not been reviewed by a lawyer and are not legal advice.

## What is new

### Security fixes

- **Overlay protection.** The scores-mode "I agree" and "Allow reading" buttons ignore taps delivered under another app's window, like every other consent control.
- **Parent lock.** The theme-change reveal ends before the parent lock is drawn.
- **HTML tag stripping** takes linear time on unclosed tags.
- **Pairing.** The child's pairing sheet shows the safety pictures. New pairings refuse an invitation that carries no parent key.
- **Critical buttons** ignore taps for 0.4 s after they appear and while a page fade or theme reveal is running.
- **Scores reports** are applied in send-time order. Replays are refused, and a report sent more than 29 days before it arrives is refused.
- **Parent passwords.** New families need a parent password of 12 or more characters.
- **Scoring engine.** Exceptions at the native boundary are caught. A conversation is marked for review only after the scoring process dies on it twice in a row.

### Bug fixes

- The screen keeps refreshing after a quick trip to the Home screen.
- An Error in the import or model-copy thread now ends as a failure instead of staying stuck.
- An export in which every chat is older than 14 days says so.
- Heartbeat interval: 30–65 minutes while the 15-minute background job is the only sender, otherwise 30–95 minutes. The parent's 100-minute limit is unchanged.
- Retry clears the relay client's back-off and reconnects.
- An over-long conversation is scored on the newest messages that fit.
- A child phone whose clock moved back no longer voids a whole scores report; the affected times are clamped.

### Collection (manual upload)

- A short bilingual guide sheet and a button that opens Instagram's own "匯出你的資訊 / Export your information" page. The child still does the export inside Instagram's own screens.
- The file picker opens at Downloads and lists zip, JSON and HTML files. The import still checks the file's content.
- After a successful import, analysis starts at the next charge without waiting for the phone to be idle. It still runs only while charging.
- A weekly, content-free reminder on the child's phone when the last successful import is more than 7 days old.
- The parent's scores page shows the date of the latest analysis with a new score.
- Optional weekly export to the child's own Google Drive or Dropbox, using Instagram's own feature, behind a confirmation dialog. IG Guard never sees the Instagram password.
- The parent can send a content-free "ask for a fresh export" request: at most once per 24 hours from the parent, shown on the child's phone at most once per 20 hours. The parent's button shows when it was used, when it can be used again and the time left. The parent is not told whether the request was delivered or read.

### Look

- Light mode is "Morning sky": blue `#1565C0` on `#E2EAF3`, navy `#12345A` cards where the app asks for a decision, and a solid blue selected tab. Dark mode "Clear Sky" is unchanged.
- The system font replaces the bundled Manrope.
- On first run the app follows the phone's dark mode until a theme is chosen.
- Themed dialogs, one back link at the top of sub-pages, text links, one choice control and notice cards for empty, loading, error and warning states.
- Plainer notification and status wording.
- The child's decision cards sit at the top of the page.

### Documents

- Terms revision `2026-11-23-v7` describes the export guide, the reminder, analysis at the next charge, the optional repeating export and the parent's request. The privacy policy, deletion guide and data-flow note say the same.

## What is unchanged

No feature, consent step, warning or security measure was removed. The Beacon model is the same. The relay code is unchanged.

## Installing and upgrading

- **Upgrading from 5.0 or 5.1 keeps your data.** 5.2 is signed with the same key as 5.0 and 5.1. Update both phones together.
- **Terms notice.** Existing users see the one-time "條款已更新" notice. Nobody is asked to consent again.
- **Mixed versions.** A 5.1 child phone ignores the parent's new request. Both phones need 5.2 for it.
- **Install limit (also true for 5.0 and 5.1).** On phones where Google Play Protect's enhanced fraud protection is active, installing the downloaded APK by opening it from a browser, messaging app or file manager is **blocked**, with no "install anyway" option. The app declares an accessibility service and a notification listener, which that protection blocks. Google says it is rolled out in many markets. It was seen on a Hong Kong tablet on 21 November 2026. Installing over USB with `adb` from a computer is not blocked: `adb install -r IG-Guard-5.2.apk`. There is no Google Play listing.
- The APK is over 1 GB because it contains the model. Space and first-start copy: see the [5.0 notes](5.0.md).

## Open risk

The child-phone impersonation risk (audit M2) is open in 5.2. Anyone holding the invitation text can impersonate the child phone: send fake heartbeats and fake scores, and knock the real child phone off the relay. The planned fix, a signing key on the child device, is not in 5.2, because it needs changes to the pairing flow. See [security-review.md](../security-review.md).

Lower-severity findings from the same review also remain open:

- The 0.4-second tap delay covers the app's own buttons but not the buttons of its confirmation dialogs.
- The four safety pictures are still derived from the family secret alone. Only the full safety code also covers the parent's key, so compare the code, not just the pictures.
- The release build runs tests and signing in the same job, without dependency verification.
- The relay has no per-address rate limit for requests to unknown rooms. This can cost the operator money; it exposes no data.

## Tested, and not tested

Tested:

- 788 unit and Robolectric tests ran on the Windows test PC.
- 1 test failed there: the known `RelayOutboxTest > concurrentFirstOpenCannotRaceDatabaseCreation`, which fails the same way on tag 5.1 on that PC (6 of 6 runs on each). The same test passed in the GitHub builds of 5.1 on Linux.
- Lint reported 0 errors.
- Two real devices (LG G7+, Android 8, child; Xiaomi tablet, Android 15, parent) ran a locally built, debug-signed copy of this code with the model inside, talking to the production relay: fresh install, first-run consent, pairing by invite link with matching safety pictures and code on both phones, scores mode with manual upload and the child's agreement, the guide sheet, the cloud-export confirm dialog (cancelled), the picker opening at Download, import of an invented export (2 chats, 27 messages), a repeat import (0 new), an all-old export and an oversized file (correct messages), a forced scoring run (about 90 seconds, no crash), scores reaching the parent (80/100 and 0/100, as in 5.1), the parent's request arriving as a card and notification with the guide button and dismissal working, the 24-hour lock on the parent, and every tab in both roles in both themes with no crash. The owner looked at light mode on both screens.
- The new button opened Instagram's own "匯出你的資訊" page in the Instagram app. The owner confirmed by photo the option "匯出到裝置" and that Google Drive and Dropbox are listed there as taking "所有可用資訊".

Not tested:

- The released 5.2 APK itself was not tested on a phone. The device checks used a debug-signed local build of the same code.
- An unforced full scoring run on a wall charger. On a PC USB port, Android stopped the after-import job after 27 seconds as "not charging".
- No real Instagram JSON export was imported, and guide steps 3 to 6 were not checked against Instagram's screens.
- Whether Instagram's link parameters pre-fill the export tool.
- The optional cloud export links are untested past the confirm dialog.
- The weekly reminder.
- The request limits (the child's 20-hour limit and the parent's 3-day line) are unit-tested only, not tested on devices.
- The "Allow reading" button and the password-confirm dialogs for single-tap feel.
- The quick-Home refresh fix on a device.
- The line under the parent's locked request button (when it can be used again and the time left) was added after the device checks; it is covered by unit and render tests only.
- Animations-off mode.
- Phones other than these two.
