# IG Guard 4.9-beta.3

Date: 16 November 2026 (Hong Kong). Terms revision: `2026-11-16-v1`.

Each phone now requires first-launch consent before role setup, family pairing, capture or relay transfer. Previously configured phones also require acceptance on first opening this version. The offline native page displays the exact bilingual [Terms](../ig-guard-terms-of-service.md) published in this repository, explains message forwarding, local and Cloudflare protections and retention, and requires three unticked acknowledgments. The signed-APK verification gate compares bundled Terms with the repository byte for byte.

Decline or Android Back opens Home and closes the task without saving acceptance. Background capture and delivery remain disabled; existing archives and pending queues are preserved. On acceptance, a device-bound AES-GCM receipt records revision, document SHA-256, time and acknowledgments. Ordinary launches and updates do not repeat the initial page. Settings → 使用條款與同意記錄 shows the original receipt and current bundled Terms. App data clearing/uninstallation removes acceptance. Unreadable receipts fail closed and are not silently replaced.

This installation consent does not grant Android permissions, enable expanded two-way capture, establish lawful guardian authority or constitute Meta approval. Existing separate signed-parent/local-child scope confirmation remains required. Material new scope needs separate consent; an old receipt does not record acceptance of future Terms. Both phones should be updated.

The family-only restriction prohibits stranger/partner/workplace/school surveillance. Cap.486 s52 has a limited statutory scope; the Terms do not promise that it exempts the developer, hosting providers or every use. Encryption is not a guarantee against a compromised phone or copies outside the app. See the [Privacy Policy](../ig-guard-privacy-policy.md), [data flow](../privacy-data-flow.md) and [deletion instructions](../ig-guard-data-deletion.md).

Verification and physical-device limits are recorded in [verification notes](4.9-beta.3-verification.md).
