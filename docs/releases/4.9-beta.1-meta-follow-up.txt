We are preparing the local IG Guard 4.9-beta.1 Android beta for authorized private
parent–child safeguarding. This version removes the typed Instagram comment and
story-reply sender and its debounce state, and does not read DM composer bodies
for outgoing filtering. Composer contents and descendants are excluded from
screen capture. Older draft records remain historical, with sending unconfirmed,
until explicitly deleted; updating does not automatically erase them.

Incoming-alert mode remains. Expanded capture of exposed incoming/outgoing DMs,
including ordinary trusted-contact conversations, requires a signed parent request
and local acknowledgment on the teen's phone. There is no separate expanded-capture withdrawal switch.
Stopping new capture requires disabling both Android notification access and
accessibility. Withdrawal does not erase pending or delivered copies.

Capture uses Android notifications and accessibility and is view-dependent and
incomplete. Encrypted delivery uses a Cloudflare relay without the family
decryption key. Buffered packets expire seven days after queuing or are removed
when consumed; retained phone records may be resubmitted. Registration metadata
has no automatic expiry or deletion route in the current relay code. Parent
archives remain until deleted. Provider logs/backups require separate verification.

Use is limited to Instagram accounts meeting the applicable minimum age, at least
13 or higher where required. Android grants and local acknowledgment are technical
controls, not proof of legal consent or authority in every case.

We request guidance on applicable platform requirements and whether a supported
official integration is available for this use. We do not claim Meta approval,
official API access or an implemented portability integration. Any future change
would require separate technical and policy review.
