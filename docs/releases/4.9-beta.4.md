# IG Guard 4.9-beta.4 — security hardening

Documented: **19 November 2026 (Hong Kong)**. Approved design: [17 November security hardening spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-security-hardening-design.md). Bundled Terms document revision: `2026-11-19-v3`.

These notes describe this version and its known limits. They do not claim completed device verification, legal certification, Meta approval or an absence of defects. Earlier release notes and reports describe their dated source versions.

## Parent access and signing

Parent UI and sensitive actions require native Android authentication: strong biometrics/device credentials on supported Android versions, with device-credential confirmation on older supported versions. An Android screen lock is required. Leaving the foreground relocks the UI and dismisses sensitive dialogs; secure-window flags request screenshot/preview protection. Private notifications use generic text. Background receipt, decryption, archiving, signed acknowledgments and eligible delivery continue while the parent UI is locked. Someone who can use the device's enrolled credentials may gain access.

Production parent-command signing uses non-exportable Android Keystore EC identities. A verified legacy import retains the existing public key trusted by the child, and the production caller removes the encoded private-key preference only after verification. Missing, unreadable or mismatched identities fail closed without silent replacement. Hardware backing depends on the device. Software identity helpers and encoded-key compatibility used in tests are not product storage guarantees. Parent password storage remains encrypted where existing reveal/pairing functions need it; the child retains a verifier.

Routine remote key rotation still uses the old channel and keeps history and eligible queued delivery within their existing deadlines. It does not recover from compromise or provide forward secrecy. The authenticated parent screen **家長主密碼與金鑰 → 秘密可能外洩：重新安全配對** creates fresh family secrets and a fresh Keystore signing identity. **先刪除舊中繼房間，再建立新配對** asks the relay to retire the old room first; the current relay cannot do this, so the app says so plainly and keeps the pairing until a relay that supports it is deployed. **無法連線：只建立新配對** requires separate confirmation and gives no assurance that old relay ciphertext was deleted. Old local archives remain isolated; old pending content and trust settings are discarded. First establish that both phones are safe and stop old capture locally on the child phone; then re-pair in person and compare the full security code. Replacement secrets do not travel over the suspect old channel, and copied old data cannot be recalled.

Explicit app copies are marked sensitive and scheduled for cleanup after 60 seconds only if that clipboard item remains current. Process termination or Android clipboard restrictions can prevent cleanup. Android selected-text copies, clipboard history, recipient apps and exported copies require separate handling.

## Child scope and stopping

On the child phone, **擷取範圍與停止** persistently stops new capture and local pending transfer, choosing discard or hold within the original deadline. Explicit resume discards old pending content and restarts incoming alerts; expanded capture needs renewed child acknowledgment. Separate outgoing and ordinary trusted-contact-context choices apply within acknowledged expanded capture. Saving choices discards old-scope pending content, and widening scope clears the acknowledgment. A trusted-contact safety alert can still qualify when ordinary trusted context is disabled. Composer drafts remain excluded.

Android notification/accessibility grants are separate. Disabling both removes both capture paths, but permission revocation alone does not discard the queue or provide the in-app transfer hold. Already forwarded or relay-buffered content may still reach the parent. Delivered archives, Instagram content and external copies need separate deletion.

## Retention boundaries

| Copy or metadata | Updated source behavior |
| --- | --- |
| New parent archive records | Default 30-day deadline from record time, capped at insertion time; settings offer 7 or 30 days. Ordinary saved/review flags do not extend it. Cleanup runs during archive access/processing and a persisted daily Android job, which Android may defer. |
| Explicit individual preservation | Record details offer **明確保留供檢視（一次，90 天）** for a one-time 90-day period from that action, with no repeated renewal. |
| Existing archives | No immediate expiry on upgrade. Preview the count that would expire, then explicitly confirm adoption. Adoption uses original record times and can immediately expire old content. |
| Phone outboxes and incomplete assemblies | 72-hour deadlines. Existing pending rows receive a 72-hour transition deadline on schema migration. Retry and routine rotation do not renew deadlines; discard/expiry identifiers prevent requeuing the same event. On-disk removal depends on cleanup running. |
| Relay packets | Unchanged: buffered packets expire seven days after first queuing, or are removed sooner when consumed. Duplicate submissions do not renew the deadline. A phone may resubmit a still-unconfirmed record after relay expiry. |
| Relay registration | Unchanged: no automatic expiry or deletion route in the relay code. The app's retirement request (HTTP DELETE) is not implemented by this relay and is expected to be reported as unconfirmed. |
| Local tombstones | Local content-free tombstones and statistics can remain after deletion. |
| Provider/support copies | Cloudflare logs, regions and backups and support-email retention remain unverified; app/relay deadlines do not cover them. |

These are logical retention controls, not guaranteed forensic erasure of flash storage, backups or copies outside the app. Relay retention is unchanged; local archive deletion does not itself purge a relay room. See [deletion instructions](../ig-guard-data-deletion.md), [privacy](../ig-guard-privacy-policy.md) and [storage security](../local-storage-security.md).

## Compatibility and experiment boundary

Existing incoming alerts, acknowledged expanded capture, watchlist/mute handling, archives, search, saved/review states, trusted contacts, pairing, temporary unlock, routine rotation and background delivery remain part of the app, subject to the deliberate authentication, consent and retention gates. Update both phones together and verify legacy public-key trust. Existing acceptance receipts keep their original revision/hash; the new document is not retroactive acceptance of revised Terms. Capture remains dependent on Instagram notifications/views and can be incomplete or uncertain.

No new model runs in the stable app. LiquidAI/d1-omni-600M feasibility work belongs to a separate experiment using synthetic scenarios; these notes do not claim that experiments ran, that Android feasibility was measured, or that grooming detection efficacy was established.

## Final integration and verification

Verified locally on 18 November 2026 (Hong Kong): **315/315 Android regression/security tests**, **25/25 local relay tests**, **33/33 build/release/config/UI checks**, and a successful debug APK build on the original hardening branch. After integration with the CI/relay security branch (relay unchanged from that branch) the integrated tree passed **354/354 Android tests**, **16/16 relay tests**, **46/46 Python build/release/config/UI/workflow checks**, and lint with **0 errors and 75 warnings**; warnings remain and are not an assurance of defect-free behavior. The debug APK version is `4.9-beta.4`; its bundled Terms match the repository document byte for byte. Existing acceptance receipts retain their original revision/hash.

Focused debug sessions covered Keystore migration and failure handling, locked/cancelled/stale parent access, encrypted navigation restoration, withdrawal/scope controls, retention and replay, retirement authorization, interrupted recovery replay, and frames expiring or being discarded while awaiting socket readiness. Independent source review findings were fixed and a bounded follow-up requested no further fix. Tests use Robolectric/JCA test providers and local Miniflare/WebSocket fixtures; they do not establish real-device Keystore or biometric behavior.

No Android device was connected. Before distributing this beta, verify native credential/biometric flows, legacy identity import, background delivery/restart, recovery/re-pairing and expiry on real parent/child devices. Relay-side retention is unchanged by this update: packets expire after seven days, room registrations do not expire and the relay cannot delete a room. The app cannot guarantee protection on a compromised phone or recall previously copied data.
