# IG Guard 4.9-beta.1

Date: **12 November 2026 (Hong Kong)**. Status: **local beta; no release publication or email sending performed by this documentation change**.

## Capture and historical records

- Remove the typed comment/story-reply sender and its debounce state. Do not read DM composer bodies for outgoing-message filtering. Screen traversal excludes composer contents and descendants; layout/hint metadata may still identify a DM screen.
- Keep incoming-alert mode and signed-parent-request plus local-child-acknowledgment two-way mode. Two-way mode can include exposed incoming/outgoing DMs and ordinary trusted-contact conversations. Updating alone does not enable it.
- Keep old draft archive records readable and labelled historical, with sending unconfirmed. Do not automatically delete them or relabel them as sent messages. Old pending copies can still arrive. Earlier release notes, reports and correspondence describe older versions and remain historical records.

## Notice and withdrawal

The [README](../../README.md), [Privacy Policy](../ig-guard-privacy-policy.md), [Terms](../ig-guard-terms-of-service.md), [Deletion Instructions](../ig-guard-data-deletion.md) and [data-flow description](../privacy-data-flow.md) explain the revised scope and retention.

To stop new capture, disable both IG Guard **Notification access** and **Accessibility** in Android Settings on the child's phone. The scope screen currently has no separate switch to withdraw expanded capture. Disabling access does not delete pending ciphertext, delivered archives or external copies; pending delivery/retry may continue. Delete existing local data separately on each phone.

Buffered relay packets expire seven days after first queuing or are removed sooner when consumed. A phone can resubmit a retained unconfirmed record after expiry. Room registration metadata has no automatic expiry or deletion route in the current relay code. Provider logs/backups and support-correspondence retention have not been independently verified.

## Limits and platform status

Use only with Instagram accounts meeting the applicable minimum age: at least 13, or higher where required. Capture depends on notifications and the view Instagram exposes. Missed history cannot be restored; labels, delivery and protection can be incomplete. No 100% guarantee is made.

IG Guard has no Meta approval. Official Instagram API and portability integrations are not implemented. The [dated follow-up draft](4.9-beta.1-meta-follow-up.txt) is unsent and does not establish approval or previous delivery.

This change adds no media classifier or new restricted permission. Existing pairing, encryption, relay, local archive and other safeguarding functions remain within their existing scope.
