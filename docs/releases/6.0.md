# IG Guard 6.0 — QR pairing and child device key

Documented: **24 November 2026 (Hong Kong)**. Bundled Terms revision: `2026-11-24-v8`. Earlier version: [5.2](5.2.md). Later version: [6.0.2](6.0.2.md).

Tag and version name `6.0`. Version code: 100 plus the GitHub Actions run number of the release run (at least 151); the installed app shows it. The release page and the APK file are named after the tag ("IG Guard 6.0", `IG-Guard-6.0.apk`).

6.0 replaces the chat invitation with a one-time QR code scanned in person, gives the child phone its own signing key, locks the relay room to that key, and warns the parent when the child's records stop arriving. These notes do not claim legal clearance, Meta approval, reliable detection of grooming or an absence of defects or vulnerabilities. The Terms have not been reviewed by a lawyer and are not legal advice.

## What is new

### Pairing

- **A one-time QR code, in person.** The parent phone shows the code ("加入子女 · Add a child") and the child phone scans it. It is new each time, valid for 3 minutes and works once. It holds a one-time secret and the parent phone's public key, and no long-term secret. The two phones then agree a new family secret between themselves; it is never shown, typed or sent through a chat app.
- **A typed check.** The child phone shows 6 digits. The parent types them on the parent phone (3 tries), accepts, confirming with the parent phone's own screen lock or biometric, and the child confirms on its own phone. Four pictures are shown on both phones as a visual aid only. If two phones answer the same QR code, pairing stops.
- **No more chat invitations.** New pairings cannot use the invitation text or link, and a child phone refuses one.
- **Cancel.** A pending pairing can be cancelled and paired again; its relay room is retired.
- **Camera.** The child phone asks for camera permission only on the scan screen, only to read the QR code. Frames are processed in memory; no image is stored or sent. The parent phone does not ask for it.

### Child device key and signed records

- **The child phone has its own signing key** in the Android Keystore; it cannot be exported. Every record it sends to the parent (heartbeats, alerts, captured messages, scores reports, acknowledgements) is signed with it.
- **The parent refuses unsigned or wrongly signed records** from a family paired by QR code. Nothing from such a record is stored, and the parent gets a notice without content, at most one per hour: "收到一則未能核實來自此子女手機的訊息，已拒絕。內容未被保存。" A correctly signed record that is too old, dated too far ahead or of an unexpected kind is dropped quietly. A forged heartbeat no longer counts as "running" for a verified child.
- **The relay locks the room to the child's key.** For a family paired by QR code the relay stores the child phone's public key (no content, no private key) and admits a child connection only with proof of that key. A connection without that proof cannot knock the real child phone off the relay.

### What this does to the risk 5.2 listed as open

5.2 listed the child-phone impersonation risk (audit M2) as open: "Anyone holding the invitation text can impersonate the child phone: send fake heartbeats and fake scores, and knock the real child phone off the relay."

- **Closed, for families paired by QR code in 6.0.** There is no invitation text to hold. Fake heartbeats and fake scores without the child phone's key are refused, and the relay does not let a connection without that key replace the real child phone.
- **Not closed, for families still on a 5.2 pairing.** They keep the old behaviour, including this risk, until both phones are paired again by QR code. The parent shows them as "not verified" (see below).
- **Not covered by the fix:** a rooted or compromised child phone (it can still use its key), a parent who pairs a phone that is not really the child's, a child who scans a stranger's QR code, and the other limits listed in [security-review.md](../security-review.md).

### Delivery

- **"Records not delivered" warning on the parent.** A child phone paired by QR code reports in its signed heartbeat how many of its records still wait for the parent's receipt, how long the oldest has waited, and whether one expired undelivered. No content and no sender. When records wait for an hour or more, or one expired, the parent shows, at most once an hour: "有子女手機的回報超過 1 小時仍未送達此手機。請檢查兩部手機的網絡；如持續出現，請當面查看子女手機。 / Reports from the child phone have not reached this phone for over an hour. Check both phones' network; if it continues, check the child phone in person." It normally can take up to about 2.5 hours after delivery stops, and at most about 3 hours plus one heartbeat interval if the parent phone's own connection keeps dropping. Control records such as capture acknowledgements are not covered.
- **Scores reports are confirmed by the parent.** The parent sends a signed receipt for each scores report it has verified and stored. The child marks a report's scores as sent only when that receipt arrives; until then the report counts towards the warning above, and scores in a lost report go out again in the next one. A 5.2 pairing works as before.

### Fixes found in device testing

- **Password check at the Join press.** When an already paired child joins a new QR pairing, the parent master password is checked off the main thread. The Join press no longer freezes the screen (about 2 seconds on the LG G7), and taps made meanwhile can no longer close the 6-digit page unseen. Password checks run strictly one at a time across the app, and attempts are counted under one lock, so the five-attempt lockout cannot be raced. While a check runs, the button and the password box show "核對中… · Checking…".
- **First pairing lands on the normal pages.** A phone's first pairing used to leave the child on the setup page (back arrow, no status line, no bottom navigation). It now finishes when the pairing is stored: the child lands on the permissions page and sees "已完成家庭配對。". The notification-access explanation is not opened automatically; granting notification access stays the child's own action on that page.

## What is unchanged

No consent step, existing warning, capture scope or security measure was removed; the new notices are the "not verified" banner, the refused-record notice and the records-not-delivered warning. The Beacon model and the scores-only mode are the same as in 5.2. The relay code changed (child keys, room locking); see "Installing and upgrading".

## Installing and upgrading

- **5.2 families keep working after the update.** Alerts, heartbeats and old-style scores reports from a 5.2 pairing are still accepted. The parent shows a permanent notice until the two phones are paired again by QR code: "此子女手機未核實 · This child phone is not verified", with "重新配對以核實 · Pair again to verify". The child phone says: "此手機在舊版本配對，家長手機會顯示為「未核實」。請在家長手機選擇「加入子女」，再用此手機掃描 QR 碼（需要家長主密碼）。" To pair again, the child phone needs the parent master password.
- **Update both phones.** A parent on 5.2 cannot show a QR code, and a child on 6.0 refuses chat invitations.
- **The relay and the apps go together.** QR pairing needs the 6.0 relay. Deploy the relay (from `main`) before pairing phones by QR code.
- **Terms notice.** Existing users see the one-time "條款已更新" notice for revision `2026-11-24-v8`. Nobody is asked to consent again.
- **Signing.** Upgrading keeps your data only if the 6.0 APK is signed with the same key as 5.2. The release workflow checks for the same signing certificate as for 5.2.
- **Install limit (also true for 5.0 to 5.2).** On phones where Google Play Protect's enhanced fraud protection is active, installing the downloaded APK by opening it from a browser, messaging app or file manager is **blocked**, with no "install anyway" option. Installing over USB with `adb` from a computer is not blocked: `adb install -r IG-Guard-6.0.apk`. There is no Google Play listing.
- The APK is over 1 GB because it contains the model. Space and first-start copy: see the [5.0 notes](5.0.md).

## Open risk

The full list of known limits is in [security-review.md](../security-review.md), under "What is not done or not protected". The most important ones:

- Families still on a 5.2 pairing keep the impersonation risk until they pair again by QR code.
- A rooted or compromised child phone can sign whatever it likes. The app cannot prove that the key sits in tamper-resistant hardware.
- The app cannot tell which phone is in front of the parent, and a child who scans a stranger's QR code pairs with that stranger.
- Relay metadata (room ids, sizes, timing, and now one child public key per room) is visible to the relay operator.
- The records-not-delivered warning does not cover every record, can take hours, and pauses while a child clock that was set back catches up.
- The relay has no per-address rate limit for requests to unknown rooms. The release build still runs tests and signing in one job.

The scores still do not reliably detect grooming. A score is a signal, not a conclusion.
