# IG Guard 4.9-beta.2

Date: 16 November 2026 (Hong Kong).

- Encrypt preference strings, including parent credentials, family secrets and the encoded control-signing key, with device-bound Android Keystore AES-GCM.
- Encrypt archived message content and labels, partial assemblies and capture-window state. Sender/thread indexes use keyed hashes; timestamps, categories, opaque IDs and workflow flags remain visible metadata.
- Migrate existing settings and records without changing the family pairing or deleting the archive. Keep keyword search and existing review, grouping, paging and pending-delivery behaviour.
- Fail closed on unreadable encrypted storage and show recovery guidance. Do not reset credentials or erase records automatically. Password input is excluded from saved activity drafts.
- Prepare remediation of historically exposed developer signing keys, including public history/log/artifact cleanup. The current trusted signing identity is preserved; copied old signing keys cannot be recalled or made safe by changing their passwords.

See [storage design and recovery limits](../local-storage-security.md), [privacy policy](../ig-guard-privacy-policy.md) and [data flow](../privacy-data-flow.md). Both phones should use the updated build. Installing source changes alone does not update existing phones. Physical-device migration and hardware Keystore behaviour are not certified by local JVM tests.

See [local verification](4.9-beta.2-verification.md) for the tests, build checks and limits.
