# IG Guard Privacy Policy

Updated: **16 November 2026 (Hong Kong)**. This notice describes IG Guard **4.9-beta.3**, with initial terms revision **2026-11-16-v1**. Safe Guard operates the project and can be reached at [ppefyp2026@gmail.com](mailto:ppefyp2026@gmail.com).

## Initial choice on each phone

Each phone displays the complete bilingual [Terms of Service](ig-guard-terms-of-service.md) offline on its initial consent page. The APK bundles the exact UTF-8 GitHub document as `ig-guard-terms-of-service.md` and displays it verbatim. The three confirmations start unticked, in this order: reading and agreeing to the full terms; conditional encrypted transfer to the dedicated verified paired parent or legal guardian with the disclosed security and retention; and authorized family-only use with the child/parent informed, without treating section 52 as automatic legal permission. Acceptance requires all three and an explicit Accept action. A separate local `ig_guard_consent` preference store holds an AES-GCM-encrypted receipt containing the acceptance date, app version, terms revision and SHA-256 of the exact terms bytes. It is not an automatic support upload or a certification of legal authority.

Collection and relay are disabled until that phone accepts. Decline or Android Back returns to Android Home without creating an acceptance receipt. Existing local queues are held pending acceptance, not erased. Previously forwarded relay or parent copies are not deleted by declining. A parent phone and a child phone each make their own initial choice; a parent's acceptance does not provide consent on behalf of every correspondent.

Normal later launches and updates do not automatically repeat this initial page. Settings provides read-only review; materially new scope needs separate consent. Acceptance agrees to the disclosed processing; it does not grant Android permissions or enable expanded capture. Pairing, Android permissions, and the signed parent request and separate child-phone acknowledgment for expanded capture still apply. Existing expanded-scope acknowledgments are retained. If existing Android permissions remain active, acceptance may resume previous capture and queued delivery; users may otherwise need to re-enable Android access.

## What IG Guard does

IG Guard pairs a teen’s Android phone with a dedicated parent or legal guardian’s Android phone for authorized private family safeguarding. Surveillance of strangers, partners, staff, students or clients, and commercial or institutional surveillance, is prohibited. After initial acceptance, pairing and the relevant Android permissions in Child Mode, the app processes Instagram notifications and selected text exposed on the phone’s screen. It uses that information to flag selected words and messages from contacts outside the family’s approved list, forward selected records encrypted to that paired parent or legal guardian for decryption and reading, and report whether protection and delivery are working. Acceptance supplies no missing pairing or Android permission, but may resume capture where pairing and active Android permissions already exist. The app uses Android services for capture. Instagram passwords and Meta API access tokens are outside this flow.

Use is limited to Instagram accounts meeting Instagram's applicable minimum age: at least 13, or higher where required. IG Guard is independent of Meta and has no Meta approval. Official Instagram API and portability integrations are not implemented.

In incoming-alert mode, the app sends qualifying incoming alerts. Expanded two-way capture requires a signed request from the parent device and a separate acknowledgment on the teen’s device. It can include exposed incoming and outgoing direct messages, including ordinary conversations with approved contacts. Version 4.9-beta.1 removes the typed comment/story-reply sender and does not read DM composer bodies to filter outgoing messages. Screen capture excludes composer contents and descendants; layout/hint metadata may identify a DM screen. Captures depend on the notifications and view Instagram exposes, so records may be incomplete or have uncertain author or account labels. There is no guarantee of 100% capture or protection.

Older builds could forward unsent comment/story-reply drafts. Those retained records remain labelled historical, with sending unconfirmed. The update does not automatically delete them; normal archive deletion controls still apply.

To stop new capture, turn off both IG Guard **Notification access** and **Accessibility** in Android Settings on the teen's phone. Turning off only one leaves the other capture path available. The scope screen currently has no separate switch to withdraw expanded capture. Disabling access does not erase pending ciphertext or delivered records; existing queues may still be delivered or retried. Parents can read delivered text and choose to copy it elsewhere. Those copies are managed by whoever holds them.

## Storage, retention and deletion

- **Parent phone.** Captured records have no automatic expiry and stay until the parent deletes them or the app’s local data is removed. Archive deletion can leave chart counts and record identifiers used to prevent deleted items from reappearing. Captured message content and account/conversation labels use AES-GCM field encryption with a device-bound Android Keystore key; HMAC lookup indexes support grouping. Timestamps, categories, opaque IDs and workflow flags remain visible metadata. This protects fields rather than encrypting the entire SQLite file. Preference strings, including the parent password, family secret and encoded control-signing key, are also encrypted at rest. The app decrypts data during authorised use. Android app isolation and device security remain necessary. Existing records migrate when opened; the update does not erase copies made earlier. Losing the device's Keystore keys can make local data unreadable, and the app does not silently reset it.
- **Teen’s phone.** Encrypted records awaiting confirmation have no fixed time expiry and can remain indefinitely and retry while pending. Before initial acceptance they are held without relay delivery; acceptance allows normal retry. Clearing IG Guard’s app storage removes the local queue, settings and pairing data from that device.
- **Consent receipt on each phone.** The local receipt has no automatic expiry; it persists across normal launches and updates. Clearing that phone's app data or uninstalling removes it. Archive-record deletion does not withdraw acceptance or erase the separate receipt.
- **Cloudflare relay.** Each buffered encrypted packet expires seven days after it is first queued and is removed sooner when consumed. Duplicate submissions do not extend that stored copy's expiry, but a phone can resubmit a retained record after expiry. Room registration metadata (parent public key, family scope and child capability hash) has no automatic expiry or deletion route in the current relay code. Nonce replay entries have a validity deadline and are pruned on subsequent parent authentication, rather than by a timed metadata purge. Contact Safe Guard for a request involving relay data; local deletion does not send a relay erasure request.
- **Provider and support records.** Cloudflare logging, hosting regions and backups have not been independently verified. No fixed support-correspondence retention period has been verified. The seven-day packet expiry does not establish those records' lifetimes.

For device controls and help with data held by the project, see the [Data Deletion Instructions](ig-guard-data-deletion.md).

## Family use and Hong Kong law

Section 52 of Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) concerns personal data held by an individual and concerned only with the management of his personal, family or household affairs, or so held only for recreational purposes. It exempts qualifying data from the Data Protection Principles, Parts 4 and 5, and sections 36 and 38(b), rather than every provision of the Ordinance. Whether a particular use qualifies depends on its facts. It does not automatically exempt Safe Guard's or Cloudflare's processing. Encryption and a family-use label do not settle that assessment.

Explain actual capture and forwarding to the teen and obtain the authority and consents required for the circumstances. Other correspondents retain applicable rights and privacy interests; other laws and Instagram's contractual rules continue to matter. Neither this notice, initial acceptance, Android permission nor a scope acknowledgment is legal certification or blanket permission to process others' information. See the [Terms of Service](ig-guard-terms-of-service.md) for the same use limits and the [data-flow description](privacy-data-flow.md) for technical boundaries.
