### 2C. 模型

模型名為「Beacon」（Qwen3 1.7B 加上經訓練的附加部分，約1.1 GB），由 llama.cpp 在子女手機的處理器上運行。原始碼儲存庫不含模型檔。在儲存庫 GitHub Releases 頁面發佈的 App 已內附模型，模型檔亦作為發佈附件公開，任何人都可以下載。其他版本的 App 沒有模型，未有模型前不能使用 AI 風險分析。子女手機首次啟動時，App 會把模型複製到其私人儲存空間（除已安裝的 App 外，另需約1.1 GB 可用空間）。手動匯入只作補救用途。檔案大小及 SHA-256 不符即被拒絕。記憶體少於約5 GB 或處理器不支援的手機，會顯示此模式不適用；實際上，售賣時標示6 GB 或以上記憶體的手機一般符合。

模型以虛構對話、開發者提供的少量真實對話、PAN-12 研究語料庫（公開的真實聊天研究資料集）的對話，以及開發者自己的普通聊天訓練及測試。這些真實資料不以檔案形式存於 App 或儲存庫內，對話亦不是以檔案形式存於模型中；但訓練後的模型有時可能重現訓練文字的片段（包括對話中其他人所寫的文字）。開發者做過一次有限度的測試，沒有發現模型重現真實的訓練對話；測試只用一種設定和一種提示長度，沒有包括 PAN-12 對話，也無法檢驗他人能否推斷某段對話曾用於訓練，故不能證明模型絕不會重現訓練文字。由於模型公開讓任何人下載，這個風險更大：取得檔案的人可以嘗試引出這些片段，而已下載的副本無法收回。使用真實對話訓練的合法依據及所需同意，仍是有待香港私隱律師確定的問題。

模型兩個方向都可能出錯，亦不能可靠分辨成年人之間的性對話與誘騙。沒有證據顯示它能可靠偵測真實的誘騙。分數只是訊號，不是結論。如認為子女有即時危險，請致電999（香港）。

### 2D. 配對、子女手機的簽署金鑰及相機

**以 QR 碼配對。** 新的配對只用 QR 碼：父母手機顯示一次性 QR 碼，子女手機當面掃描。QR 碼每次都是新的，3分鐘內有效，只可使用一次；內含一次性秘密及父母手機的公鑰，不含任何長期秘密。掃描後，兩部手機之間自行協定新的家庭秘密；該秘密不會顯示、輸入，亦不會經聊天程式傳送。子女手機會顯示6位數字，父母須在父母手機輸入（最多3次），再按「接受」，並以父母手機本身的螢幕鎖或生物辨識（指紋、圖案或 PIN）確認；子女在自己的手機確認。兩部手機亦顯示四個圖案，只作視覺輔助。如有兩部手機回應同一個 QR 碼，配對即停止。聊天邀請（配對文字或連結）不再用於新的配對，子女手機會拒絕它們。

**此版本之前的配對。** 之前已配對的手機繼續運作；父母手機會持續顯示「未核實」提示，直至兩部手機再以 QR 碼配對。

**子女手機的簽署金鑰。** 子女手機在 Android Keystore 建立自己的簽署金鑰，不可匯出。子女手機傳給父母的每一則記錄（心跳、提醒、擷取的訊息、分數報告、確認回覆）都以此金鑰簽署。父母手機以此方式核實某部子女手機後，沒有該金鑰有效簽署的記錄會被拒絕，其內容不會儲存，父母會收到一則不含內容的通知（最多每小時一則）。簽署有效但過舊、日期設在太遠的未來或類型不符的記錄，會被靜默丟棄。子女手機的已簽署心跳另帶兩個計數及一個旗標，說明有多少回報仍未確認送達父母手機、最舊的一則已等候多久，以及是否曾有回報逾期仍未送達（不含內容，不含發送者）；父母手機據此顯示一則不含內容的提醒，但由回報停止送達起計，提醒通常最長約 2.5 小時後才出現（心跳每 30 至 95 分鐘一次，另加 60 分鐘的等候規則）；如父母手機本身的連線不斷中斷，最長約 3 小時再加一次心跳的間隔。

**中繼站儲存甚麼。** 對以 QR 碼配對的家庭，中繼站儲存子女手機的公鑰（不含內容，不含私鑰），並只接受能證明持有該金鑰的子女連線。未以此方式配對的家庭，處理方式不變。

**相機。** 子女手機只在掃描畫面為讀取 QR 碼才請求相機權限。影像只在記憶體中處理，不儲存，不傳送。父母手機不請求相機權限。

**仍然沒有保護的情況。** 已 root 或已被入侵的子女手機仍可使用其金鑰。父母若配對了並非子女本人使用的裝置，或子女掃描了陌生人的 QR 碼，此機制不能防止。子女解除安裝 App 時，父母只會看到心跳缺失。中繼資料仍然可見。如子女手機時鐘調快，而惡意中繼站扣起一則已簽署的心跳，該心跳其後最多可延遲24小時仍被視為最新。App 不能證明金鑰存放於防篡改硬件。此機制不保證沒有漏洞，亦不保證偵測到所有偽造；詳見儲存庫的 docs/security-review.md。分數報告及其他控制記錄（例如擷取確認）不等待父母手機確認收妥，未送達提醒不涵蓋它們。

### 3. 加密及保留

### 1. Family only

Only authorized private safeguarding by parents/legal guardians is permitted. No surveillance of strangers, partners, staff, students or clients, or commercial/institutional monitoring. Meet Instagram's minimum age (at least 13), applicable law and terms. Explain scope, recipients and stopping to the teen; obtain required authority/consents. Each phone accepts separately; parents cannot consent for all correspondents.

### 2. Conditional forwarding

Acceptance agrees to the processing below; it neither grants Android permissions nor enables expanded capture. With Child Mode pairing, Android permissions and collection active, selected Instagram notification/screen text is encrypted through Cloudflare to the dedicated verified paired parent/legal guardian for reading, including text, account/conversation labels, direction, times and alert reasons. Baseline sends qualifying incoming alerts. Expansion separately requires a signed parent request and local child acknowledgment, covering incoming messages, sent outgoing messages and ordinary trusted-contact context within the child's choices. Outgoing and trusted-context scope can be chosen separately; widening scope requires renewed acknowledgment. Composer drafts are excluded; historical drafts remain labelled unconfirmed as sent and subject to archive controls. This forwarding does not apply in the scores-only mode of section 2A: once the child agrees to that mode, the parent receives no message text.

A child phone checks in in the background about every 15 minutes: it sends a heartbeat when one is due and asks the relay for parent commands. This runs even when both Notification access and Accessibility are off. Android may delay it under battery saving.

### 2A. AI risk analysis (scores-only mode)

This mode is optional and off by default. The parent can switch it on only after the child's phone has reported a valid model (see 2C). The child must agree on their own phone; the parent cannot agree for them. Nothing changes before the child agrees, and choosing "Not now" stores and analyses nothing. This agreement is separate from the three confirmations in section 7. Every parent change to this mode (on, off or method) needs a fresh device authentication on the parent phone.

**What happens.** One-to-one conversations are analysed on the child's phone by a local model, only while the phone is charging. Chats read from the screen cannot always be told apart from group chats, so a group chat opened on the phone may be analysed; a chat known to be a group is not analysed and is only counted. The child phone's encrypted store keeps the latest 60 messages per conversation and nothing older than 14 days. Each conversation is scored on its latest 30 messages.

**What the parent receives.** For each conversation: the other person's account label, a score from 0 to 100 or "needs review", and the time. Also alerts without message text, whether analysis is on, paused or withdrawn, and the model's state. In this mode the parent does not receive message text, even in an emergency. Score reports travel encrypted through the relay like other records.

**Two-way capture options.** In this mode the two-way capture options only decide what the child phone itself stores for analysis. They never send text to the parent. With automatic capture, analysis runs only after the child has confirmed the two-way capture scope.

**The child's controls.** The child can pause at any time. Storing and analysis stop at once and the parent is told; stored messages are kept, and resuming needs no new agreement. The child can also withdraw. This deletes the stored chat text on the child's phone and the parent is told. Alerts then stay without text until the parent switches the mode off.

**The parent switches the mode off.** The child's phone stops analysing and deletes the stored chat text, the child is told, and the earlier mode applies again (except during a capture hold, see 2B). An existing family's archive from before the mode stays on the parent phone until the parent deletes it.

**Deletion.** The stored chat text on the child's phone is deleted when the parent switches the mode off, the child withdraws, the model is removed, the collection method changes (when the child agrees to the new one), the child stops capture, the role or family changes, or the room is deleted. Score reports already sent stay on the parent phone. They hold no message text but do hold the other person's account label, which is personal data. On the parent phone, scores expire with the archive's retention period, counted from when they were scored, and **Delete all evidence for this family** also deletes them.

**The other person in each conversation.** Their messages are stored and analysed on the child's phone, and their account label is sent to the parent. They have not been asked. The parent's or child's agreement does not speak for them.

### 2B. Collection method

The parent chooses the method, and the child must agree on their own phone. A change of method needs the child's fresh agreement. Until then the previously agreed method stays in effect; with no earlier agreement, nothing is collected for analysis.

**Automatic capture.** The app reads Instagram notifications and conversation screens on the child's phone (Notification access and Accessibility). The parent must tick this warning before the choice is sent, and the child's consent page repeats it:

> **Automatic capture may breach Instagram's Terms of Use**
> This method reads Instagram notifications and conversation screens on your child's phone. Instagram's Terms of Use prohibit collecting information in an automated way without its permission. Your child's Instagram account could be restricted or disabled as a result. IG Guard is independent of Meta and has no Meta approval. By choosing this method you confirm that you understand this risk and that the decision to use it, and the responsibility for it, are yours. If you do not accept this, choose Manual upload.

This warning tells you about a risk. It does not by itself remove anyone else's legal responsibility, and it does not change sections 5 and 6. Keyword and unapproved-contact alerts keep working, without message text.

**Manual upload.** The app reads nothing from Instagram: no notification or screen is stored, scored, forwarded or alerted on. The teen imports the file from Instagram's own "Download your information" (a .zip, or one message_N.html / message_N.json). The file is read as a stream and is not copied into the app. Only message text, direction, time and conversation label of one-to-one chats are kept, in the child phone's encrypted store; group chats are skipped. The same limits apply: the latest 60 messages per conversation, nothing older than 14 days. Real-time keyword and unapproved-contact alerts are not available in this method. The file is supplied by the child, so the parent cannot know that it is complete or unedited. The app cannot switch off Notification access or Accessibility for the child.

**Capture hold.** If the child agreed to manual upload and the parent later switches the scores-only mode off, the app does not go back to reading Instagram. Capture stays off until the child allows it on a card on their own phone, and the parent is told that capture is held.

**Export guide and link (manual upload).** The child phone's upload card links to the steps for Instagram's own "Export your information" tool and has a button that opens the tool's fixed address (https://accountscenter.instagram.com/info_and_permissions/dyi) in the browser or the Instagram app. The address carries no data about the child or the family. The child does the export inside Instagram's own screens; the app does not operate Instagram for the child and does not read Instagram's screens. The file picker opens at the Downloads folder and lists zip, JSON and HTML files; this is a convenience only, and the app still checks the file's content.

**Weekly reminder.** While manual upload is agreed and analysis is on (not paused, not withdrawn), if the last successful import (or, with none yet, the agreement) is more than 7 days old, the child phone shows at most one reminder a day asking the child to refresh the Instagram export. The reminder appears on the child phone only, contains no message content and is not sent to the parent. For this, the child phone's encrypted store keeps only the time of the last successful import, which is deleted together with the stored chat text. On Android 13 or later it appears only if the app is already allowed to show notifications.

**Parent's request for a fresh export (manual upload).** While manual upload is agreed and analysis is on (not paused), the parent can ask the child from the scores page to refresh the Instagram export, at most once every 24 hours. The request carries no content: it is a signed command and its time. The parent is not told whether it was delivered or read. The child phone shows one notification and a card only while an import is possible there (manual upload agreed, analysis on, not paused, not withdrawn), and ignores a request that arrives within 20 hours of the last one shown. The child may ignore it or dismiss it with "OK"; nothing is blocked or limited, and the child is not penalised, if the child ignores it. The child phone's encrypted store keeps only the time of the last request shown and whether its card was closed; the card goes when the child dismisses it or after a successful import, and the time stays to limit repeats and is deleted together with the stored chat text. If no newer score arrives more than 3 days after a request, the parent's scores page only says that this may simply mean there are no new messages. This needs and asks for no Accessibility or Notification access; on Android 13 or later the notification appears only if the app is already allowed to show notifications.

**Analysis when charging after an import.** After a successful import, the child phone starts analysing the newly imported chats at the next charge, without waiting for the phone to be idle. Analysis still runs only while charging; pausing, withdrawing, switching the mode off or changing the method cancels this.

**Optional repeating export (Meta's feature).** The guide sheet also offers optional links that open Instagram's own "Export to external service" set-up to export messages every week to the child's own Google Drive or Dropbox. This is Meta's feature, not IG Guard's. Once set up, a copy of the messages sits in the child's own cloud drive under that provider's terms and must be deleted there separately. Instagram asks for the Instagram password; IG Guard never sees or receives it. IG Guard does not read that cloud drive; the child still imports by picking the file in the same file picker. Before a link opens, the app shows this explanation and asks for confirmation. This has not been tested on a Hong Kong teen account; Instagram may not offer it or may not include messages.

### 2C. The model

The model is "Beacon" (Qwen3 1.7B with a trained add-on, about 1.1 GB), run by llama.cpp on the child phone's processor. The source repository does not contain the model file. The app released on the repository's GitHub Releases page includes the model, and the model file is also published there as a release asset, so anyone can download it. Other builds of the app have no model; on them AI risk analysis cannot be used until a model is present. On first start on the child's phone the app copies the model into its private storage, which needs about 1.1 GB of free space on top of the installed app. Manual import is a recovery path only. The app refuses any file whose size and SHA-256 do not match. Phones reporting less than about 5 GB of memory, or with an unsupported processor, show the mode as unavailable; in practice phones sold with 6 GB or more qualify.

The model was trained and tested on invented conversations, a small number of real conversations supplied by the developer, conversations from the PAN-12 research corpus (a public research collection of real chats), and the developer's own ordinary chats. None of that real material is stored as files in the app or the repository, and the conversations are not stored as files inside the model. A trained model can sometimes reproduce fragments of its training text, including text written by other people in those conversations. A limited test by the developer found no reproduction of the real training conversations; it used one setting and one prompt length, did not include the PAN-12 conversations, cannot show whether someone could tell that a particular conversation was used in training, and cannot prove that the model never reproduces training text. Because the model is published for anyone to download, this risk is greater: anyone with the file can try to draw such fragments out, and a downloaded copy cannot be recalled. The lawful basis and consent for using real conversations in training is an open question for a Hong Kong privacy lawyer.

The model can be wrong in both directions. It cannot reliably tell sexual conversation between adults from grooming, and nothing shows that it reliably detects real grooming. A score is a signal, not a conclusion. If you believe a child is in immediate danger, call 999 (Hong Kong).

### 2D. Pairing, the child phone's signing key and the camera

**Pairing by QR code.** New pairings use only a QR code: the parent phone shows a one-time QR code and the child phone scans it in person. The code is new each time, valid for 3 minutes and works once. It holds a one-time secret and the parent phone's public key; it holds no long-term secret. After the scan the two phones agree a new family secret between themselves; it is never shown, typed or sent through a chat app. The child phone then shows 6 digits, the parent types them on the parent phone (3 tries), and then accepts, confirming with the parent phone's own screen lock or biometric (fingerprint, pattern or PIN); the child confirms on its own phone. Four pictures are also shown on both phones as a visual aid only. If two phones answer the same QR code, pairing stops. Chat invitations (the pairing text or link) are no longer used for new pairings, and a child phone refuses them.

**Pairings made before this version.** They keep working. The parent phone shows a permanent "not verified" (未核實) notice until the two phones are paired again with the QR code.

**The child phone's signing key.** The child phone creates its own signing key in the Android Keystore; it cannot be exported. Every record the child phone sends to the parent (heartbeats, alerts, captured messages, scores reports, acknowledgements) is signed with it. Once the parent phone has verified a child phone this way, a record without a valid signature from that key is refused, its content is not stored, and the parent gets a notice without content (at most one per hour). A correctly signed record that is too old, dated too far ahead, or of an unexpected kind is dropped quietly. The child phone's signed heartbeat also carries two counters and one flag about reports not yet confirmed as delivered to the parent phone (how many, how long the oldest has waited, and whether one ever expired undelivered), with no content and no sender; the parent phone uses them to show a notice without content, which normally can take up to about 2.5 hours after delivery stops (a heartbeat every 30 to 95 minutes plus the 60-minute rule), and at most about 3 hours plus one heartbeat interval if the parent phone's own connection keeps dropping.

**What the relay stores.** For a family paired by QR code, the relay stores the child phone's public key (no content, no private key) and admits a child connection only with proof of that key. Families that were not paired this way are handled exactly as before.

**Camera.** The child phone asks for camera permission only on the scan screen, only to read the QR code. Frames are processed in memory; no image is stored or sent. The parent phone does not ask for camera permission.

**What is still not protected.** A rooted or compromised child phone can still use its key. This does not prevent a parent pairing a device that is not really the child's phone, or a child scanning a stranger's QR code. If the child uninstalls the app, the parent only sees missing heartbeats. Relay metadata remains visible. If a child phone's clock is set ahead and a hostile relay holds a signed heartbeat back, that heartbeat can count as fresh later by at most 24 hours. The app cannot prove that the key sits in tamper-resistant hardware. This mechanism does not promise that the app is free of vulnerabilities or that every forgery is detected; see docs/security-review.md in the repository. Scores reports and other control records (such as capture acknowledgements) do not wait for the parent phone's receipt, so the undelivered-reports notice does not cover them.

### 3. Encryption and retention

Transport uses AES-256-GCM. WSS/TLS terminates at Cloudflare; payloads remain family-encrypted. Safe Guard/Cloudflare process ciphertext and room/delivery/connection metadata without the family decryption key. Phone preference strings and archive content/labels use Keystore AES-GCM field encryption and HMAC indexes. Times/categories/opaque IDs/status remain visible; the whole database file is not encrypted. Parents can read/copy content; secure phones, passwords and pairing secrets.

The parent UI requires Android biometric/device-credential authentication and relocks on leaving the foreground; background receipt, decryption, archiving and acknowledgments continue. Secure-window flags and generic private notifications reduce screen exposure. Explicit app copy actions mark clipboard items sensitive and attempt to clear the still-current item after 60 seconds; other copies cannot be recalled. Production parent signing identities use non-exportable Keystore private keys. Verified legacy import preserves the public key already trusted by the child before removing the old private-key preference. If moving an existing key into the Keystore fails, the app keeps using that existing key (stored encrypted by the app, not hardware-protected), shows a warning and retries at each start; the old copy is removed only after the Keystore key is verified to match the key the child trusts. Hardware backing depends on the device; unavailable keys are not silently replaced. Routine remote rotation is not recovery from key compromise. **秘密可能外洩：重新安全配對** creates fresh secrets and signing identity; first establish that both phones are safe, stop the child's old capture locally, then re-pair in person with the QR code (the parent types the 6 digits shown on the child phone). Replacement secrets do not travel through the suspect old channel. The screen can first send the child phone a signed release and ask the relay to delete the old room, and reports whether the relay confirmed it; if it did not, the existing pairing is kept and creating a new pairing only remains available, without assurance that the relay has deleted old data. Old local archives stay isolated, old pending content and trust settings are discarded, and copied old data cannot be recalled.

New archives receive a 30-day expiry counted from when the parent's phone receives the record by default (the time shown comes from the child's phone and does not affect expiry); parents can choose a shorter period. Record details offer explicit preservation for a one-time 90-day period, not indefinite renewal; the ordinary saved/bookmark flag does not extend expiry. Existing archives are not immediately expired/deleted on upgrade: preview and confirmation are required to adopt retention. Phone pending content and incomplete assemblies receive 72-hour deadlines, unchanged by retries or routine rotation. Expired content is cleaned during app activity and archive processing; immediate on-disk removal of all content at the deadline is not assured while the app is stopped.

The relay expires buffered packets seven days after first acceptance, or removes them sooner when consumed; duplicates do not extend the original deadline. If neither the parent's nor the child's phone connects for 90 consecutive days, the relay deletes that room's registration metadata (including the child phone's public key stored for a family paired by QR code) and any packets still waiting. An authenticated parent can also ask in the app for the room to be deleted. A deleted or expired room is retired for good: the relay deletes its registration metadata and waiting packets at once and keeps only a retirement marker that holds no key, name or message and has no expiry. The room, its key and the child phone's credential cannot be used again, and both phones must be paired afresh. Before a parent deletes a room, the app first sends the child phone a signed release; a child phone that verifies it removes that room's key and unsent records. A child phone that is offline or does not receive the release is cleared only when it is paired again. The parent phone discards the old key and unsent records when it creates the new pairing; its saved archive is kept. The relay also keeps content-free packet records (identifier hash, body hash, type and deadline) for up to 90 days after a packet's deadline, to prevent the same record being restored through replay. Local replay identifiers and statistics can remain. Deleting a room does not delete the parent's archive, content already delivered or any other copy; deleted/expired identifiers help prevent restoration of the same record through replay and do not establish erasure of every copy. Provider logs, regions, backups and support-email retention remain unverified and are outside these deadlines.
