# IG Guard Terms of Service

Updated: **12 November 2026 (Hong Kong)**, for the IG Guard **4.9-beta.1 local beta** operated by Safe Guard. You can reach the project at [ppefyp2026@gmail.com](mailto:ppefyp2026@gmail.com).

## Who may use IG Guard

Use IG Guard only for private family safeguarding with a parent or legal guardian who has authority to set up the app on the teen’s device. Use it only with Instagram accounts that meet Instagram’s applicable minimum age, which is at least 13 and may be higher where the account holder lives. Follow applicable law and Instagram’s terms when setting up and using the app.

Explain to the teen what the app can read, what can be shared with the paired parent and how to stop monitoring. Obtain any permissions and consents required for the circumstances. Enable expanded two-way capture only after explaining that it can include outgoing messages and ordinary conversations with trusted contacts and after the teen has acknowledged the scope on their phone.

Version 4.9-beta.1 removes unsent comment/story-reply capture and excludes composer contents from screen capture and outgoing-message filtering. Historical draft records from earlier builds remain labelled as historical and unconfirmed as sent; they are not automatically deleted. Android permission grants and the in-app acknowledgment do not alone establish lawful authority or legal consent.

## Handling captured information

Use captured information only for the family safeguarding purpose explained during setup. Limit access to authorized people. Keep the parent password, pairing invitations and both devices secure. Consider the privacy of everyone whose messages may appear, including conversation partners. Follow applicable law when copying, retaining or sharing a record.

## Limits of alerts and delivery

IG Guard captures only the text available through the enabled Android services. Capture depends on what Instagram shows, the permissions and state of each phone, and network and relay availability. Alerts can be late or incomplete, and labels can be inaccurate. There is no guarantee of 100% capture, delivery or protection. A keyword or contact alert is a prompt for a person to review the context. Use local emergency or safeguarding services for urgent help.

IG Guard is an independent project. Instagram is a separate service operated by Meta and subject to its own terms. IG Guard has no Meta approval. Official Instagram API and portability integrations are not implemented. Possible future integrations are plans, not current capabilities or permission to use Instagram contrary to its terms.

## Stopping use and removing data

Stop new capture by turning off both IG Guard's notification access and accessibility service in Android Settings on the teen's phone. The scope screen currently has no separate switch to withdraw expanded capture. Disabling access does not erase queued, delivered or externally copied data; pending records may still be delivered. Remove app data on each phone separately. The [Privacy Policy](ig-guard-privacy-policy.md) describes retention, including relay metadata that has no automatic expiry, and the [Data Deletion Instructions](ig-guard-data-deletion.md) explain removal and privacy requests.

Your applicable statutory rights and remedies continue to apply.
