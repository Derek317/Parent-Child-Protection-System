## Terms 2026-11-20-v5: AI risk analysis, collection method and model (20 November 2026, Hong Kong)

No app version is changed by this entry. The text has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not legal advice.

- The Terms (new sections 2A to 2C), privacy policy and deletion guide now describe the scores-only mode (AI risk analysis): the child's own agreement, analysis on the child's phone while charging, what the parent does and does not receive (no message text, even in an emergency), pause and withdraw, and which events delete the stored chat text while score reports stay on the parent's phone. They replace the earlier "no new model runs in the stable app" sentence. The 19 November drafts in `docs/drafts/2026-11-19-scores-only/` were the starting point and stay unmerged drafts.
- They describe the two collection methods, with the bilingual automatic-capture warning word for word, the manual-upload limits (the app reads nothing from Instagram; no real-time keyword or unapproved-contact alerts; the parent cannot know an uploaded file is complete or unedited), the capture hold, and the 15-minute background check-in.
- They describe the Beacon model, how it arrives, the memory limit (about 5 GB reported) and what it was trained on, including real conversations and PAN-12; the lawful basis for the real conversations stays marked as an open question. No claim of Meta approval, legal clearance or reliable detection is made.
- People who accepted an earlier revision see a one-time 條款已更新 notice with a link on their next visit; nothing is re-asked and their original receipt is kept. The child's separate scores-mode agreement is unchanged by this revision.
- Terms section 3 and the first-run summary no longer say the relay cannot delete a room or that its registration never expires: the recovery screen can send the child a signed release and ask the relay to delete the old room, reporting whether it confirmed, and a registration is deleted after 90 days without a connection or when the parent deletes the room.
- `docs/privacy-data-flow.md` stays a dated description of 4.9-beta.3 and now points to these documents for the new mode.

## Scores-only family mode (19 November 2026, Hong Kong) — source work on branch `app/local-risk-scoring`, not released

No app version is changed by this entry. Nothing below has run on a physical phone, and the model was tested mostly on invented chats and on a small number of real ones. On the real testing chats it flagged 6 of 10 grooming chats and 0 of 37 ordinary chat windows; those chats also helped choose the model, so this is not a fresh test, and it fails the handoff 90-chat set (27/36, 5/36). It is not shown to detect real grooming reliably.

- Add an optional scores-only family mode. The parent switches it on only after the child's phone has reported a valid model file; the child sees a plain-language page and must agree once. Until the child agrees, the earlier behaviour continues unchanged. The child can pause (stops storing and scoring at once) or withdraw (deletes the local store and unsent scores). Turning the mode on again needs agreement again.
- While the mode is on, the child's phone keeps the latest 60 messages per one-to-one thread for up to 14 days in an encrypted local store, scores each thread on its latest 30 messages while charging and idle (charging only after 48 hours without a run), and sends the parent only an account label, a 0 to 100 score or "needs review", and times. Message text is no longer sent to the parent in this mode. Chats read from the screen (kind unknown) are analysed too. A chat known to be a group, or one that shows a second other person in a notification, is set aside: what was kept is deleted and it is only counted. The screen records no author for incoming messages, so a group chat opened on the phone may be analysed, and the consent wording says so; the consent wording changed, so a child who agreed earlier is asked again; until they agree again nothing is stored or analysed, no message text is sent, alerts stay without text, and the screens say so.
- Keyword and unapproved-contact alerts keep working but carry only the category, the account label and the time, marked as sent without text. After a withdrawal the phone does not go back to forwarding text; only the parent switching the mode off does, and the child is told when that happens.
- Importing or removing the analysis model on the child's phone needs the parent's password.
- Add an optional locally built package: `tools/package_with_model.py` (Python standard library; steps in `docs/local-model-package.md`) takes the release APK and the model on Derek's own PC, checks the model against the size and SHA-256 pinned in `RiskModel.kt`, stores it uncompressed as `assets/risk/<file name>`, runs zipalign, signs with v2 and v3 and verifies. On a child phone whose model is missing, the app copies that asset into its private storage by itself (size and SHA-256 checked, free space checked first, no parent password, no change to consent or mode). Removing the model still needs the parent's password, and the included copy is then installed again only after the parent enters it. Manual import is unchanged for APKs without the asset. The packaged APK contains the model and must never be uploaded, committed or shared. `/dist/`, `*.apk` and `*.apk.sha256` are now git-ignored.
- Add the on-phone scoring engine (llama.cpp, CPU, arm64-v8a) and a hand-imported model file that is refused unless its size and SHA-256 match exactly. The pinned file is Beacon (`qwen3-1.7b-beacon-Q4_K_M.gguf`, 1,107,408,736 bytes, SHA-256 `5b873dbf…bb5c`), chosen by Derek on 20 November 2026; the earlier v3, v4 and v7 files are no longer accepted. Beacon was trained on invented chats, a small number of private real transcripts supplied by Derek, windows from the PAN-12 research corpus and Derek's own ordinary chats; only the invented chats are in the repository, so it cannot be rebuilt from the repository alone. The model file is never part of the repository. The native host check ran on the base model, not on Beacon. Phones reporting under about 5 GB of memory (in practice, phones sold with less than 6 GB), or where the library cannot load, report the mode as unavailable.
- Add parent and child screens: a score page with states for waiting, paused, withdrawn, out of date, model missing or wrong, and scoring; a child status block that is always visible, and a permanent notification line while analysis is on, paused or waiting.
- The Terms of Service, privacy policy and data-flow drafts for this mode are drafts awaiting review by Derek and a Hong Kong privacy lawyer; the live documents are unchanged. The wording makes no claim of Meta approval or legal clearance.
- Not verified: speed, memory, heat and battery on a real phone, how often idle-and-charging windows occur, importing a 1.1 GB file through the system picker, installing a 1.1 GB APK, the first-start copy of the included model, packaging with the real model, and screen-reader behaviour on real hardware.

## 4.9-beta.4 — 19 November 2026 (Hong Kong)

- Add native Android biometric/device-credential gating for parent UI and sensitive actions, relock on background, secure-window flags and generic private notifications. Parent background receipt, archiving and signed acknowledgments continue while the UI is locked. Explicit app copies are marked sensitive and get best-effort cleanup after 60 seconds if still current.
- Move production parent signing to non-exportable Android Keystore identities. Verified legacy import preserves the public key already trusted by the child and removes the encoded private-key preference only after the Keystore key is verified; if the move fails, the existing encrypted key keeps working and the move is retried; unavailable keys never silently replace established trust. Add separate fresh, locally verified compromise re-pairing; the current relay cannot delete a room, so only creating a new pairing is offered; routine old-channel rotation remains separate.
- Add persistent child capture/transfer withdrawal with hold/discard choices and separate outgoing/trusted-context choices. Explicit resume discards old pending content and restarts incoming alerts; expansion needs renewed child acknowledgment. Saving scope choices discards old pending content, and widening scope clears acknowledgment. Composer drafts remain excluded.
- Assign new archives a default 30-day expiry and pending outboxes/incomplete assemblies 72-hour deadlines. Add an explicit one-time 90-day preservation action in record details and preview/confirmation before legacy archives adopt retention. Ordinary saving does not extend retention; cleanup runs during app activity rather than promising instant deletion while stopped.
- **Relay.** Buffered packets still expire seven days after first queuing, and resubmission can no longer extend that. Room registrations now expire after 90 days without a connection from either phone, and an authenticated parent can delete a room. A deleted or expired room is retired for good and its key cannot be used again; only a content-free marker is kept. Rooms registered before this change keep working. In-app consent and help texts state the relay's 7-day packet lifetime and the phone's 72-hour outbox and 30-day archive deadlines separately.
- Update bundled bilingual Terms, privacy, deletion and storage guidance. Preserve the existing feature set subject to deliberate security gates. No new model runs in the stable app; synthetic-data model experiments remain separate. Packaging/revision synchronization and final implementation verification remain outstanding.
- **Fresh authentication.** Showing the parent master password, routine key rotation, remote child reset, compromise re-pairing and leaving the parent role each ask for a new biometric/device-credential check; an open session is not enough. Cancelling cancels only that action. Leaving the parent role is now an explicit, authenticated step that removes the master password and parent signing key from the phone (stored records stay).
- **Pairing links.** A parent phone, or an unconfigured phone that still holds parent secrets, refuses `igguard://pair` with a plain message and never deletes the master password or signing key because of a link. A child phone can be re-paired only by entering the current parent password (checked with the existing verifier and lockout) or after a signed remote reset; a temporary unlock alone is no longer enough. A tamper alert is queued to the old family before the switch, and delivery is awaited for up to 6 seconds; if the relay is unreachable that notice cannot be sent after the switch. A pairing link at a locked child phone raises at most one alert per hour.
- **Temporary unlock and time.** Unlock expiry, unlock-code life and the 10-minute wrong-password lockout are counted on `elapsedRealtime` with a boot marker; the wall-clock expiry stays as an additional upper bound. An unlock ends at reboot, a reboot restarts (never ends) a running lockout, and moving the clock back cannot extend an unlock or revive a code. Unlock codes are single-use. A child whose clock moves back by more than 5 minutes sends one tamper alert per hour. A rolled-back clock still makes the child reject signed commands; that protocol limit is unchanged.
- **Diagnostics.** Checkpoint logs no longer record sender names or Instagram handles; older entries are rewritten on read, and deleting all evidence clears the logs.
- **Overlay protection.** Consent accept, pairing and re-pair confirmation, two-way capture confirmations, remote reset, role switch, leaving the parent role and delete-all/recovery confirmations ignore touches delivered while another window covers them and say so; ordinary buttons keep working with screen dimmers and overlays.
- **Parent key migration cannot cut the parent off.** If importing the signing key into the Android Keystore fails, the parent keeps signing and authenticating with the verified existing key, shows a warning and retries at the next start; the old key is removed only after the Keystore key is proven to match the key the child trusts. The verified identity is cached per process.
- **Evidence retention follows the parent's receipt time.** The child's clock only affects the displayed time, so setting the child's date back cannot make alerts expire on arrival. Expired outbox lifetime rows are removed once their outbox rows are gone.
- **Leaving without stranding the child.** Leaving the parent role and compromise re-pairing show the master password, explain that the child stays bound until reset, and by default send a signed reset to the child (waiting up to 8 seconds for the relay to accept it; the child cannot confirm, because it becomes unconfigured on receipt). An unconfigured phone can remove leftover parent data in place after a fresh device authentication.
- **The parent hears about the child leaving.** A child that switches to unconfigured with the parent password, or stops collection, queues one content-free notice first and waits up to 6 seconds for the relay; nothing is sent afterwards.
- **More actions need a fresh authentication:** delete all evidence, delete other families' archives, retention changes and legacy adoption, sharing or copying the pairing invite, and generating an unlock code.
- **Room deletion.** Deleting a room sends the child phone a signed release first; a child that verifies it removes the old room's key and unsent records, and one that is offline is cleared when it is paired again. The parent's saved archive is kept. A deleted room is reported in plain words with a request to pair again, and is asked about at most once a day. A record the relay refuses as expired no longer holds up other alerts.
- **Terms.** Terms revision 2026-11-20-v4 states the relay's retention and room deletion (packets 7 days, registrations expire after 90 idle days, a deleted room is retired for good). People who accepted an earlier revision see a one-time 條款已更新 notice with a link; nothing is re-asked.
- The relay-room deletion option now says plainly that the current relay cannot delete a room and the pairing is kept; the other option is labelled 只建立新配對（不刪除中繼資料）.
- Pairing, relay messages and signed commands keep their wire formats; updated and not-yet-updated paired phones keep working together. Not addressed here: single-use or short-lived invitations, per-device child keys, end-to-end sequence numbers and changing how trusted senders are recognised.

See the [release notes and known limits](docs/releases/4.9-beta.4.md). Earlier entries describe their historical versions.
